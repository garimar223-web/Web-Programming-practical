Technology Definitions

Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Technology that enables machines to simulate human intelligence and perform tasks such as learning and decision-making.
Cloud Computing
Delivery of computing services such as storage, servers, and applications over the Internet.
Cyber Security
Protection of computer systems, networks, and data from cyber threats and unauthorized access.
Internet of Things (IoT)
A network of interconnected devices that collect and exchange data through the Internet.
Blockchain
A distributed digital ledger used to securely record transactions across multiple systems.
